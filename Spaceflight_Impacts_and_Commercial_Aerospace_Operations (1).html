China Dam Field & Dark-Sky Briefing

Site telemetry synthesized from HikeArizona, AZOffroad, ClearDarkSky, and Phoenix dark-sky observer reports.

Route & Access

Take Castle Hot Springs Rd north past Lake Pleasant to Cow Creek Rd, then follow Tip Top Mine Access Rd toward Humbug Creek. Multiple unmarked spider trails—use offline GPS tracks.

Vehicle & Terrain

4WD/high-clearance recommended off Cow Creek Rd (monsoon ruts reported); 2WD pickups can reach within ~0.25 mi. Upper China Dam is silt-filled and solid for parking; Main China Dam sits just downstream with its historic central breach.

Light Pollution Profile

ClearDarkSky Light Pollution Atlas places this basin north of Lake Pleasant in the Bortle 3–4 transition (21.51–21.89 mag/arcsec² at zenith). Expect a 10°–15° Phoenix/Peoria skyglow dome to the SE–S, while N–NW toward the Bradshaw Mountains remains deeply dark. Unlike Lake Pleasant Regional Park (sunset gate closure), BLM/public pulloffs stay accessible at night.

Observer Protocol

Allow 15–20 minutes for scotopic eye adaptation. Use red headlamps only, pack warm layers (desert lows drop into the 40s–50s°F), and avoid camping inside the breached creek wash.
